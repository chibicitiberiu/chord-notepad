What changed

  • Edit / Play are tabs in the app bar; ribbon is one 48 dp row.
  • Edit: no status line (messages are snackbars). If the keyboard leaves too little room, the header collapses and only the text shows.
  • Phone landscape: tabs and ribbon share one line.
  • Play: a music-player bar under the text: voicing · instrument · ▶/⏸ · metronome · speed (popup). No Stop: a playhead marker sits in the text; tap anywhere to move it to the nearest chord, drag to scrub; ▶ plays from it. Tapping a chord also plays it.
  • Play: tempo, time and key fold into one “Song” chip (♩ 90 · 3/4 · G) that opens a sheet; purple tag = value comes from a directive.
  • Capo moved to ＋ Insert (it changes fret/tab shapes, never the sound).
  • “Sheet” button removed: drag the chord sheet's handle to collapse it.

Icons

  • Pictogrammers Material Design Icons (Apache-2.0) for everything except the capo and the 3/4 time signature, which MDI lacks (ours).
