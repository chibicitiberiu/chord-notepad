== en-US ==
A small update to 0.6.0. The loop picker's count now starts at 2, since a loop that plays its section once doesn't repeat anything. A {loop: verse 1} you've typed keeps working and plays the section once.

New in 0.6.0, in case you missed it: a completely redesigned app with light and dark themes, separate Edit and Play workspaces, a player with a timeline, speed and volume, a redrawn chord sheet, find and replace, export to WAV audio and PDF, and a Suggest Capo that lists every capo position with the chord shapes you'd play.
