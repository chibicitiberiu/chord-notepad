## Title
Chord Notepad

## Short description
Write lyrics with chords, then hear each chord and learn how to play it

## Full description
Chord Notepad is a text editor for musicians. Type chord symbols above your lyrics; the app recognises them, highlights them and plays them back, voiced the way a pianist or guitarist would play them.

<b>Writing (Edit mode)</b>
• American (C, Am, Bb7), European (Do, Re, Mi) and Roman numeral (I, IV, V7) notation, from triads to slash chords and altered jazz chords
• Directives for tempo, time signature, key, capo, sections and repeats, such as {bpm: 90}, {time: 3/4}, {key: G} and {loop: verse 2}
• Transpose a song or a selection, convert between American and European notation, identify a chord from its notes, and get a capo suggestion for guitar

<b>Playing (Play mode)</b>
• Tap a chord to hear it, or play the song from the playhead while each chord lights up as it sounds
• Piano voicings that move smoothly from chord to chord, playable guitar fingerings in several tunings, and ensemble voicings for choir or strings; edit them or create your own
• 128 General MIDI instruments, a metronome, and playback speed from 25% to 400%
• A chord sheet shows the voiced chords as a piano roll, a staff, chord boxes or tab

<b>Files and privacy</b>
• Songs are plain text files, opened and saved through the Android file picker (local storage or cloud providers); songs can be exported as MIDI files
• No account, no ads and no internet permission; your songs stay on your device

Chord Notepad is free, with every feature included, and it's free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. If you find it useful, an optional one-time purchase inside the app supports its development and adds a supporter badge. The source code and free builds for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux are at https://github.com/chibicitiberiu/chord-notepad
